# Mental Health Insights Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring mental health conditions among university students, based on anxiety, depression, and panic attack reports across different courses and age groups.

## Project Overview

This project was developed to analyze patterns of reported mental health conditions among students using Power BI for data visualization and exploratory analysis.

The dashboard focuses on:
- Anxiety reports
- Depression reports
- Panic attack reports
- Distribution across academic courses
- Distribution across age groups
- Mental health trends among technology-focused programs

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1, Overview Analysis
General overview of:
- Anxiety, depression, and panic attack percentages
- Overall mental health condition distribution
- Specialist treatment seeking behavior

## Key Insights

- Technology-focused courses showed higher concentrations of reported anxiety and depression.
- Younger students, especially aged 18–19, presented the highest concentration of reported mental health conditions.
- Anxiety was one of the most frequently reported conditions among students.
